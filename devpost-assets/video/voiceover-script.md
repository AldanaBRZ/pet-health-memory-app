# Continuum Demo Voiceover

The finished silent video is `continuum-live-demo-silent.mp4`. It runs for 2:27.5, leaving a comfortable margin under the three-minute limit.

## 0:00-0:25 - Why I built it and Today

Five years ago, I adopted Orejas from the streets of Argentina. He is six now, with recurring back, hip, and stomach problems. I kept asking: how can I remember all of this when the vet asks what changed? Continuum is the pet health memory I wished I had. Today keeps only the care that needs attention now.

## 0:25-0:36 - Timeline

Symptoms, vet visits, medication changes, physio, behavior, tests, and procedures stay together in one searchable timeline instead of scattered notes and messages.

## 0:36-0:52 - What Changed

The What Changed view summarizes the last seven, thirty, or ninety days. It gives owners a clear picture of recent events, symptoms, costs, and questions to discuss, without trying to diagnose anything.

## 0:52-1:06 - Vet Visit Mode

Before an appointment, Vet Visit Mode gathers the most useful details into one focused screen: recent changes, questions, symptoms, current medication, costs, and documents.

## 1:06-1:22 - Vet Packet

In an emergency, the Vet Packet puts allergies, conditions, medication, care instructions, microchip details, and contacts first. It can be shared, printed as a PDF, or downloaded as a standalone page.

## 1:22-1:40 - Care Plan and trends

The Care Plan keeps medication, reminders, prevention, and routines connected. Trends make changes in weight, symptoms, and monthly care costs easier to notice and explain over time. This is observation, not medical advice.

## 1:40-2:07 - Longevity and documents

The longevity layer helps owners stay on top of preventive care and everyday habits like activity, physio, grooming, appetite, and medication adherence. Documents such as bloodwork, x-rays, invoices, and discharge notes can stay beside the history they belong to. Demo data is temporary, and real account data stays private.

## 2:07-2:21 - Codex and GPT-5.6

I built Continuum through many small iterations with Codex using GPT-5.6. Codex implemented features, fixed bugs, and ran the tests. GPT-5.6 helped me reason through workflows, security decisions, medical-safety language, and revisions.

## 2:21-2:27 - Close

I built it for Orejas. I want every pet parent to be ready when the vet asks: what changed?

## Recording notes

- Begin speaking immediately when the title appears.
- Read conversationally and leave a small pause at each paragraph break.
- Keep the microphone about 15 to 20 cm away.
- Do not rush the final Codex and GPT-5.6 paragraph; both names must be clearly audible.
- Keep music absent or very quiet beneath the voice.
- After exporting, watch the complete video on a phone and confirm the voice remains clear.
