Use the local working file `video/continuum-live-demo-silent.mp4`. It is a verified H.264, 1280 x 720 interactive recording that runs for 2:27.5. The live cursor moves between real app controls, and a coral ripple expands around every click.

Upload the final voiceover mix to YouTube and use that link in Devpost.

The complete timestamped narration is in `video/voiceover-script.md`. It covers Orejas, the core product workflow, medical-safety positioning, Codex, and GPT-5.6.

### Voiceover and upload settings

- Record in a quiet room with the microphone 15 to 20 cm away.
- Begin speaking as soon as the opening title appears.
- Read conversationally and leave a small pause at paragraph breaks.
- In iMovie, CapCut, or another editor, import `video/continuum-live-demo-silent.mp4`, then record or place the voiceover over it.
- Do not add background music unless it is very quiet. Your voice should be immediately clear on a phone speaker.
- Export at 720p or 1080p with the voiceover mixed clearly.
- Watch the complete export and confirm Codex and GPT-5.6 are both clearly audible.
- Upload to YouTube as **Public** or **Unlisted**.
- Open the YouTube URL in a signed-out browser before adding it to Devpost.
